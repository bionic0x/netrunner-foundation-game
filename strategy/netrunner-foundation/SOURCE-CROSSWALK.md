## Consolidation rules

1. **Keep, do not silently rewrite:** the original texts remain historically traceable by hash. Only the new blueprint is canonical for project decisions.
2. **Prefer purpose over recency:** a later, more extreme claim is not automatically a validated upgrade; retain variants as source assertions.
3. **No duplicate evidence:** long executive compendia and short summaries often repeat the same underlying narrative; do not count repetitions as corroboration.
4. **Reclassify unsafe designs:** live hostile trading, engineered liquidation cascades, market manipulation, covert infiltration, RAT deployment, identity concealment and regulatory evasion are threat cases or rejected features; do not implement.
5. **Separate economics:** token issuance, NFT rights and DAO profit splits are alternative, unverified financial proposals requiring independent legal and arithmetic analysis.
6. **Preserve protocol independence:** Solana and Arbitrum One share only deliberately defined schema fields and review process.
