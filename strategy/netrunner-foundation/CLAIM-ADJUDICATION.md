## Blocking safety invariants

`NO_SIGNING`, `NO_CUSTODY`, `NO_TRADING`, `NO_LIVE_ATTACK`, `NO_MARKET_MANIPULATION`, `NO_UNAUTHORIZED_INTRUSION`, `NO_EVIDENCE_DESTRUCTION`, `NO_GUARANTEED_RETURNS`, `NO_CROSS_NETWORK_EVIDENCE_TRANSFER`, `NO_FAKE_LIVE_DATA`, `NO_UNSUPPORTED_ATTRIBUTION`.

`PROCEED` for documentation or replay cannot downgrade any invariant. A later product using real data needs privacy/source rights, protocol scope, authorizations and a review contract; a future financial product needs a separate decision charter.
